# AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) Exam Simulator & Cram

A mobile-first, zero-latency exam simulator and rapid-fire study app designed specifically for passing the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)** certification exam on your first attempt.

---

## 🏆 Why This Test Simulator is Better

Most practice tests available online suffer from major flaws: they contain ambiguous wording, unvetted question banks, excessive scrolling fatigue on mobile, and slow, delayed feedback. 

This application was engineered from the ground up to solve these problems and simulate the real Pearson VUE testing environment with high-yield pedagogical rigor:

### 1. Strict 1:1 Blueprint Fidelity (65 Questions per Round)
Rather than serving arbitrary batches, each round strictly matches the real AWS exam:
* **Exactly 65 Questions** per round (mirroring the 50 scored + 15 unscored questions of the real exam form).
* **Official 85-Minute Countdown Timer** with pause/resume controls and visual alerts when time runs low.
* **Exact Domain Weightings**:
  * **Domain 1: Fundamentals of AI and ML (20% — 13 Questions)**
  * **Domain 2: Fundamentals of Generative AI (24% — 16 Questions)**
  * **Domain 3: Applications of Foundation Models (28% — 18 Questions)**
  * **Domain 4: Guidelines for Responsible AI (14% — 9 Questions)**
  * **Domain 5: Security, Compliance, and Governance (14% — 9 Questions)**
* **Real AWS Scaled Scoring Simulation (100–1000)** with the official **700 passing threshold** and detailed domain breakdown.

---

### 2. Zero-Latency Pre-Computed 4-Option Analysis
* **Instant Feedback**: The moment you tap **A, B, C, or D**, the app immediately reveals whether your selection was correct or incorrect with zero network delay or API lag.
* **Why Right is Right**: A concise, punchy 2-sentence explanation citing the exact AWS service or design principle required.
* **Why Every Wrong Option is Wrong**: Explicit breakdown of why each distractor fails the business requirement (e.g. why SageMaker JumpStart is wrong when serverless API access is requested, or why Clarify cannot substitute for Bedrock Guardrails at runtime).

---

### 3. Purged of Ambiguity and Fluff
* **No Debatable or Obsolete Questions**: Purged of questions arguing over loose algorithmic definitions or confusing statistical trivia.
* **Direct Architectural Scenarios**: Questions get straight to the point, testing canonical AWS decision factors:
  * *Least operational overhead* (e.g. Managed Bedrock Knowledge Bases vs. custom EC2 scripts).
  * *Most cost-effective* (e.g. Batch Transform vs. 24/7 Real-Time Endpoints; On-Demand vs. Provisioned Throughput).
  * *Zero infrastructure management* (e.g. Bedrock serverless API vs. SageMaker JumpStart compute instances).

---

### 4. Single-Screen Mobile Ergonomics (Zero Scrolling)
* **Single Viewport Experience**: The entire question, all 4 options, and the instant breakdown fit within your phone’s screen height (`h-dvh` / `h-screen`).
* **1-Click Instant Submit**: Tapping an option immediately submits your answer and reveals the solution — eliminating the need to click "Submit" and then "Next".
* **Full-Width Tactile Controls**: Pinned 50/50 full-width **Previous** and **Next** buttons designed for effortless thumb navigation.
* **Official Light Mode**: Clean, off-white Pearson VUE test-center theme engineered for crisp outdoor legibility and testing familiarity.

---

### 5. Built-in AWS Product Confusion Killer
Targets the most common pitfalls where candidates lose easy points on the exam:
* **Amazon Bedrock vs. Amazon SageMaker JumpStart** (Serverless API vs. dedicated SageMaker hosting instances).
* **Amazon Bedrock Guardrails vs. Amazon SageMaker Clarify** (Runtime safety & PII filtering for FMs vs. offline training data bias & SHAP explainability).
* **Knowledge Bases for Bedrock vs. Amazon Kendra** (Managed RAG embeddings pipeline vs. standalone enterprise semantic search engine).
* **Amazon Comprehend vs. Amazon Textract** (Extracting sentiment/entities from text vs. extracting tables/forms from scanned PDFs).
* **Amazon Transcribe vs. Amazon Polly** (Speech-to-Text audio transcription vs. Text-to-Speech voice synthesis).
* **Amazon Q Business vs. Amazon Q Developer** (Enterprise document search & chat assistant vs. IDE coding assistant).
* **SageMaker Model Cards vs. Model Dashboard** (Governance documentation factsheet vs. live production endpoint drift monitoring).

## 🎯 How AWS Scoring Works
* **Scale**: 100 to 1,000 points.
* **Passing Score**: **700** (~70% to 72% raw accuracy).
* **Compensatory Model**: You do not need to pass each section individually. Because **Domains 2 and 3 make up 52% of the entire exam**, mastering Generative AI and Bedrock gives you a massive advantage to easily clear the 700 threshold.
* **No Penalty for Guessing**: Never leave a question blank on exam day!
